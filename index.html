Roblox Rivals Meta Tier List

The ultimate weapon rankings. Use the best, avoid the trash.

S
Primaries: Assault Rifle, Sniper Rifle, Permafrost, Paintball Gun, Minigun
Others: Revolver, Warper, Katana, Scythe, Molotov, Freeze Ray Overpowered meta. Absolutely essential if you want to win ranked matches.
A
Primaries: Energy Rifle, Flamethrower, Wildcat, Gunblade
Others: Uzi, Exogun, Flare Gun, Daggers, Medkit, Subspace Tripmine Great weapons. Highly competitive and completely reliable.
B
Primaries: Burst Rifle, Crossbow, Grenade Launcher, Shotgun
Others: Energy Pistols, Slingshot, Handgun, Battle Axe, Grenade, Satchel Decent choices but held back by long reloads or heavy damage falloff.
C
Primaries: RPG, Bow
Others: Shorty, Spray, Chainsaw, Riot Shield, Grappler, Flashbang, Jump Pad Very situational. Fun to mess around with, but easily countered.
D
Primaries: Scepter (RNG Mode Only)
Others: Trowel, Smoke Grenade Weak gear. Outclassed by almost every other option in the game.
💩
Items: Knife, Fists, Spear, Glass Shard
Utterly useless garbage. Choosing these will literally throw your matches.
